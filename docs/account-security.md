# Login, password, and two-factor authentication

## Reset your password

Click **Forgot password?** on the login page and enter your email. The reset link arrives within a few minutes from `security@pharos.example` and expires after 1 hour. If it doesn't arrive, check spam, and make sure you're using the email address your account was created with. If your workspace uses single sign-on, reset your password with your company's identity provider instead.

## Turn on two-factor authentication (2FA)

Go to **Profile > Security > Two-factor authentication** and scan the QR code with an authenticator app such as Google Authenticator, Authy, or 1Password. Save your 10 backup codes somewhere safe; each one works once. Workspace admins can require 2FA for everyone under **Settings > Security**.

## Lost your phone or 2FA device

1. Log in with one of your backup codes instead of the 6-digit code.
2. Once you're in, go to **Profile > Security** and set up 2FA again on your new device.

No backup codes? Contact support from the email address on the account. For your security, we'll verify your identity before removing 2FA: we ask for the name of your workspace and, if you're the owner, the last 4 digits of the card on file. Support agents never ask for your password.

## Account locked

After 10 failed login attempts, the account locks for 30 minutes. Wait, then reset your password if you're unsure of it.

## Suspicious activity

If you see logins you don't recognize under **Profile > Security > Recent activity**, reset your password right away, click **Sign out everywhere**, and turn on 2FA. Then contact support so we can review the activity with you.

## Reporting a security vulnerability

Security researchers can report vulnerabilities to `security@pharos.example`. Please don't post details publicly until we've had a chance to fix the issue.
