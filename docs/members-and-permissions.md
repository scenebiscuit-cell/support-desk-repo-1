# Members, guests, and permissions

## Invite members

Go to **Settings > Members > Invite** and enter email addresses. On paid plans, each new member is added to your bill with a prorated charge for the current period. The Free plan allows up to 3 members.

## Invite guests

Guests are clients or contractors who only need to see specific projects. Invite them from a project's **Share** menu. Guests are free: up to 5 per paid member on the Team plan, and unlimited on Business. Guests can view, comment, and complete tasks assigned to them, but can't create projects.

## Roles

| Role | Can do |
|---|---|
| Owner | Everything, including billing, cancelling, and deleting the workspace. One per workspace. |
| Admin | Manage members, projects, integrations, and settings. No billing access. |
| Member | Create and edit projects and tasks. |
| Guest | View and comment on projects they're invited to. |

## Transfer ownership

The current owner can transfer ownership under **Settings > Workspace > Transfer ownership**. The new owner must already be an admin. If the owner has left the company and can't be reached, contact support. We'll need proof that you're authorized to act for the business, such as a business license or a recent invoice with your name on it.

## Remove a member

Go to **Settings > Members**, open the member, and click **Remove**. You'll be asked to reassign their open tasks. Their comments and history stay. Their unused time is credited to your next invoice.

## Invite didn't arrive

Ask them to check spam for an email from `team@pharos.example`. You can resend it from **Settings > Members > Pending**. Invites expire after 7 days.
