# Billing, invoices, and refunds

## When you're charged

Monthly plans renew on the same day each month as your first payment. Annual plans renew once a year. Extra SMS charges from the previous month are added to your next invoice.

## Find your invoices

Account owners can download every invoice as a PDF under **Settings > Billing > Invoice history**. To add a company name, VAT number, or a different email for invoices, click **Edit billing details** on the same screen.

## Update your payment method

Go to **Settings > Billing > Payment method** and click **Update card**. We accept Visa, Mastercard, American Express, and Discover. If a payment fails, we retry three times over seven days and email the account owner each time. After the final failed retry, your booking page pauses until the card is updated. Your data is not deleted.

## Refund policy

- **Monthly plans:** We don't refund partial months. When you cancel, your plan stays active until the end of the period you already paid for.
- **Annual plans:** If you cancel within 30 days of an annual charge, we refund the unused portion, pro-rated by day. After 30 days, annual plans aren't refundable, but you keep access until the end of the year.
- **Duplicate or incorrect charges:** If you were charged twice or charged by mistake, we refund the full incorrect amount. Refunds take 5 to 7 business days to show up on your statement.
- **Extra SMS charges:** These are usage-based and aren't refundable, except when a charge was caused by a Pharos bug.

## Requesting a refund

Only the account owner can request a refund. Contact support from the email address on the account and include the invoice number (it starts with `PHR-`). A support agent must approve every refund; refunds can't be issued automatically.
