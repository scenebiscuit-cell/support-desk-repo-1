# Customize your booking page

## Branding

Go to **Booking page > Design** to upload your logo, choose a brand color, and add a cover photo. Changes go live as soon as you click **Publish**.

## Booking rules

Under **Booking page > Rules** you can set:

- **Minimum notice:** how far ahead clients must book (for example, at least 2 hours ahead).
- **Booking window:** how far into the future clients can book (default is 60 days).
- **Cancellation window:** how late clients can cancel or reschedule online (default is 24 hours).
- **Intake questions:** custom questions clients answer when booking, such as "Is this your first visit?"

## Custom domain (Plus plan)

On the Plus plan, you can serve your booking page from your own domain, like `book.yourbusiness.com`. Go to **Booking page > Domain**, enter the domain, and add the CNAME record shown on screen at your domain provider. DNS changes can take up to 24 hours to work. SSL certificates are issued automatically.

## Embed on your website

Copy the embed code from **Booking page > Share > Embed** and paste it into your website builder. The embed works with Squarespace, Wix, WordPress, and plain HTML.

## Booking page shows an error or won't load

1. Check the Pharos status page at `status.pharos.example` for an outage.
2. Make sure your account isn't paused or past due (a paused page shows "This business isn't taking bookings right now").
3. Try the page in a private browser window to rule out browser extensions.
4. If it still fails, contact support with the page URL, a screenshot, and the time it happened.
