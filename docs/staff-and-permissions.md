# Staff accounts and permissions

## Add a staff member

Go to **Team > Invite staff** and enter their name and email. They get an email invite to create their own login. Each staff member gets their own calendar, services, and availability. The number of staff calendars depends on your plan: 1 on Solo, up to 10 on Team, unlimited on Plus.

## Roles

| Role | Can do |
|---|---|
| Owner | Everything, including billing, cancelling, and deleting the account. One per account. |
| Admin | Manage all calendars, services, staff, and settings. No billing access. |
| Staff | Manage their own calendar and see their own clients. |
| Front desk | Book, reschedule, and cancel for any staff member. Can't change settings. |

## Transfer ownership

The current owner can transfer ownership under **Settings > Account > Transfer ownership**. The new owner must already be an Admin. If the owner has left the business and can't be reached, contact support. We'll need proof that you're authorized to act for the business, such as a business license or a recent invoice with your name on it.

## Remove a staff member

Go to **Team**, open the staff member, and click **Deactivate**. Their future appointments must be reassigned or cancelled first. Their past appointment history is kept. Deactivated staff don't count toward your plan's limit.

## Staff invite didn't arrive

Ask them to check spam for an email from `team@pharos.example`. You can resend it from **Team > Pending invites**. Invites expire after 7 days.
