# Billing, invoices, and refunds

## How seat billing works

Paid plans are billed per member. When you add a member partway through a billing period, we charge a prorated amount for the rest of that period, and the charge appears on your next invoice. When you remove a member, the unused time is credited toward your next invoice. Guests are free and don't count as members.

## Find your invoices

Workspace owners can download every invoice as a PDF under **Settings > Billing > Invoice history**. To add a company name, VAT number, or a different email for invoices, click **Edit billing details** on the same screen.

## Update your payment method

Go to **Settings > Billing > Payment method** and click **Update card**. We accept Visa, Mastercard, American Express, and Discover. If a payment fails, we retry three times over seven days and email the owner each time. After the final failed retry, the workspace becomes read-only until the card is updated. Nothing is deleted.

## Refund policy

- **Monthly plans:** We don't refund partial months. When you cancel, your plan stays active until the end of the period you already paid for.
- **Annual plans:** If you cancel within 30 days of an annual charge, we refund the unused portion, pro-rated by day. After 30 days, annual plans aren't refundable, but you keep access until the end of the year.
- **Duplicate or incorrect charges:** If you were charged twice or charged by mistake, we refund the full incorrect amount. Refunds take 5 to 7 business days to show up on your statement.
- **Prorated member charges:** These aren't refundable, but removing a member credits their unused time to your next invoice.

## Requesting a refund

Only the workspace owner can request a refund. Contact support from the email address on the account and include the invoice number (it starts with `PHR-`). A support agent must approve every refund.
