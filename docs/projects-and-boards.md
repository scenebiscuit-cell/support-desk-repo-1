# Projects, boards, and templates

## Customize a board

Add, rename, or reorder columns by clicking the column header. Drag tasks between columns to update their status. Use **Custom fields** under **Project settings** to track things like budget, client, or effort.

## Save a project as a template

Open the project, click **More > Save as template**, and choose what to include: columns, tasks, custom fields, and assignees. Templates appear when anyone in the workspace creates a new project. Templates are available on all plans.

## Private projects (Business plan)

On the Business plan, you can make a project private under **Project settings > Access** and choose exactly who can see it. On Free and Team plans, every member can see every project, but guests only see projects they're invited to.

## Recurring tasks

Open a task, click the due date, and choose **Repeat**. You can repeat daily, weekly, monthly, or on custom days. A new copy is created when the current one is completed.

## Due dates look wrong

Due dates show in each person's own time zone. If a date shows a day early or late, check the time zone under **Profile > Preferences**. Ask clients and guests to check theirs too. A task due at midnight in New York can show the previous evening for someone in Los Angeles; set a specific due time to avoid that.

## Archive a project

Click **More > Archive** on the project. Archived projects are hidden from the sidebar but keep all their tasks and history. Find them under **Projects > Archived**, and restore them anytime.
