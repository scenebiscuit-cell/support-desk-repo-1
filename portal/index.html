How it works

The Keeper is designed to point you toward the help content that supports each answer.

  1. Ask in your own words

    Your question is matched against the Pharos help-center articles.

  2. Get a grounded answer

    When an article covers it, the AI uses the matching passages and shows its source.

  3. Check the source

    Open the citation in a reply to read the exact help-center passage.

  4. Still stuck? Raise a request

    Contact support and follow the reply and status under My requests.

This is a fictional product demo. It does not access real accounts. If an answer is not in the articles, the Keeper says so and offers to raise a request. Requests are saved only in your browser, and they appear in the agent desk, where you can answer them as the support agent and see the reply land here under My requests.
