# Notifications

Pharos notifies you when you're assigned a task, mentioned, or when a task you follow changes. You can get notifications in the app, by email, and in Slack.

## Choose what you're notified about

Go to **Profile > Notifications**. For each type (assignments, @mentions, due soon, comments on tasks you follow), choose in-app, email, both, or off.

## Daily digest

Turn on **Daily digest** to get one summary email each morning instead of individual emails. When the digest is on, individual email notifications are paused.

## Not getting notification emails

Check these in order:

1. **Email is turned on** for that notification type under **Profile > Notifications**.
2. **Daily digest is off**, if you want individual emails.
3. **Do not disturb** isn't on. It's the moon icon next to your profile picture.
4. **Check spam** and add `notifications@pharos.example` to your contacts. Company email filters sometimes block automated messages; ask your IT team to allow our domain.
5. **You're following the task.** You're only notified about comments on tasks you're assigned to, mentioned in, or following.

## Getting too many notifications

Turn on **Daily digest**, switch comment notifications to in-app only, or unfollow tasks you don't need updates on. Workspace admins can set defaults for new members under **Settings > Notifications**.

## Notification history

See every notification from the last 30 days under the bell icon. If you contact support about missing notifications, include a screenshot of this list and your notification settings.
