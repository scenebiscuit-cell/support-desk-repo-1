# Troubleshooting common problems

## Tasks are missing

Tasks almost never disappear for good. Check these first:

1. **Filters:** A filter or saved view may be hiding tasks. Click **Clear filters** at the top of the board.
2. **Archived:** Completed tasks can be archived automatically. Turn on **Show archived** in the view menu.
3. **Moved:** Search for the task by name; it may have been moved to another project.
4. **Trash:** Deleted tasks stay in **Projects > Trash** for 30 days. Open the task and click **Restore**.

Workspace activity under **Settings > Activity** shows who moved, archived, or deleted a task (Business plan).

## File won't upload

Attachments can be up to 100 MB each on paid plans and 10 MB on the Free plan. For bigger files like videos, attach them from Google Drive instead. If a smaller file fails, try a different browser and check that your network doesn't block uploads.

## Pharos is slow or won't load

1. Check `status.pharos.example` for an outage.
2. Refresh the page, then try a private browser window to rule out extensions.
3. Pharos supports the latest two versions of Chrome, Edge, Firefox, and Safari.

## Reporting a bug

Contact support with what you did, what you expected, what happened instead, your browser, and a screenshot or screen recording. The more detail, the faster we can fix it.
