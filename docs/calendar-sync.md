# Calendar sync and double bookings

Pharos syncs two ways with Google Calendar and Outlook / Microsoft 365. Pharos appointments appear on your calendar, and events on your calendar block time on your booking page.

## Connect a calendar

Go to **Settings > Calendar sync > Connect** and sign in with Google or Microsoft. Each staff member connects their own calendar from their own login.

## How often it syncs

Pharos checks your connected calendar every 5 minutes. Appointments booked in Pharos are pushed to your calendar within about a minute.

## Getting double booked?

Double bookings almost always come from one of these causes:

1. **The event is marked "Free" instead of "Busy."** Only events shown as *Busy* block your booking page. Open the event in Google or Outlook and change "Show as" to *Busy*.
2. **The wrong calendar is selected.** If you have several calendars (work, personal, family), choose which ones block availability under **Settings > Calendar sync > Calendars to check**.
3. **The connection expired.** Google and Microsoft sometimes expire access after a password change. If **Settings > Calendar sync** shows a red "Reconnect" banner, click it and sign in again.
4. **The event was added in the last 5 minutes.** A client may have booked before the next sync ran.

## Reconnect calendar sync

Click **Disconnect**, wait 30 seconds, then click **Connect** and sign in again. Existing appointments aren't deleted when you disconnect.

## Still double booked?

Contact support with the date and time of both appointments, the staff member, and which calendar the conflicting event is on. We can check the sync log for your account.
